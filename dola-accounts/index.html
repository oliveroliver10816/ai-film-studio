You are creating a few separate Dola accounts and writing down some details about each one. Nothing technical. Follow the steps in order and copy the answers into the table at the end.

Before you start

Have these ready

  • A laptop or desktop computer with Google Chrome.
  • Your normal home internet. Do not use a VPN.
  • A phone that can receive text messages, in case Google asks.
  • About 20 minutes per account. Do not rush them all in one go.
Start with 3 accounts only. Make three, send them to Bob, and wait. We need to check how much free usage one account actually gets before making more. Making ten now could waste them.

The rules

Read once · they matter more than the steps

  1. Make a brand-new Gmail for each account. Never use an existing email, and never use any email that belongs to Bob or to any of his work.
  2. Every account gets its own new Gmail. Three accounts means three new Gmails. Never sign into Dola twice with the same Gmail.
  3. Do not put the same recovery phone number or recovery email on more than one Gmail. This is the most important rule on the page. If two accounts share a recovery number, Google treats them as the same person and both can be closed.
  4. One Chrome profile per account. In Chrome, click your picture at the top right, then Add, and make a new profile before each new account. Do not use Incognito.
  5. Space them out. Make one account, then wait at least two hours before making the next one. Do not make all three in the same hour.
  6. Never close or delete a Chrome profile once it is made. The saved login inside it is the part we actually need.
  7. Do not type any password into this page or into any message. Write the passwords in a private note and send that to Bob directly.

Steps, for one account

Repeat the whole list three times

  1. Open Chrome. Click your picture at the top right → Add → Continue without an account. Name the profile dola1 (then dola2, dola3).
  2. In that new profile, go to accounts.google.com/signup and make a brand-new Gmail. Give it a different recovery email or phone from the other accounts.
  3. Still in the same Chrome profile, go to https://www.dola.com/chat/.
  4. Sign in and choose Continue with Google. Pick the new Gmail you just made.
  5. Accept whatever it asks and land on the chat screen.
  6. Look for the video option and check whether it says Seedance 2.0. Write down yes or no — if it is not there, say so, that account is still useful information.
  7. Send one test message to the chat: type hello and press enter. This proves the account works.
  8. Now generate one short test video so we can see the free limit. Use the prompt in the box below, exactly as written.
  9. Write down what happened: did it generate, how long it took, and whether the screen showed any message about a limit, credits or an upgrade.
  10. Leave the Chrome profile signed in and do not close it down. Move on to the next account only after two hours.
